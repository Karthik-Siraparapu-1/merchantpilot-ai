## Video Outline & Timings

- **Visual:** Screen recorded on `http://localhost:3000/` (Landing Page).
- **Narration:** "Welcome to MerchantPilot AI — the AI Operating System for Commerce. Traditional e-commerce dashboards force merchants to manually inspect 10 different tools every morning. MerchantPilot replaces static dashboards with an autonomous digital workforce of 7 specialized AI executives."
- **Action:** Scroll through hero section, highlight badges, and click "Explore Interactive Demo".

---

### 2. Login & Multi-Tenant Switcher (0:30 – 0:50)

- **Visual:** `/login` page -> Enter `demo@merchantpilot.ai` / `Demo@123!`.

- **Action:** Click login, highlight the organization switcher in the sidebar.

---

### 3. Executive Command Center & Continuous Thinking (0:50 – 1:35)

- **Visual:** `/dashboard` (AI Command Center).
- **Narration:** "Here on the Executive Dashboard, notice the Continuous Thinking Ticker at the top. The AI workforce is actively scanning inventory velocity, competitor pricing, and fraud signals in real-time. Notice the Digital CEO Cockpit summarizing key business metrics."
- **Action:** Hover over the ticker items, point out revenue telemetry, low-stock alerts, and recent transactions stream.

---

### 4. Hands-Free Voice AI Assistant (1:35 – 2:35)

- **Visual:** Click Microphone icon or Floating AI Orb to trigger Voice AI Modal.
- **Narration:** "MerchantPilot includes a hands-free Voice AI assistant with wake-phrase detection and context memory. Let's speak with the AI."
- **Action / Prompts:**
  - Prompt 1: _"Explain my business health today."_ -> Show transcribing and reasoning state.
  - Prompt 2: _"Which products are at risk of stockout?"_ -> Voice AI navigates UI or surfaces stock analysis.
  - Prompt 3: _"Why did revenue decrease yesterday?"_ -> AI provides explainable breakdown.

---

### 5. AI Workforce Command & Multi-Agent Debate (2:35 – 3:20)

- **Visual:** Navigate to `/ai-workforce` (AI Workforce Command).
- **Narration:** "Rather than a single monolithic chatbot, MerchantPilot operates an AI Digital Employee Workforce with 7 specialized executives: Athena (Strategy), Atlas (Inventory), Vega (Pricing), Sentinel (Fraud), Pulse (Payments), Orion (Retention), and Nova (Marketing). When complex events occur, candidate actions enter the Multi-Agent Debate Engine where agents cross-examine tradeoffs before Athena issues a final verdict."
- **Action:** Click "Replay Debate" on the debate card to show live agent deliberations.

---

### 6. Digital Twin Scenario Simulator (3:20 – 4:05)

- **Visual:** Navigate to `/scenario-lab` (Scenario Lab).
- **Narration:** "Before executing major changes, merchants can run Monte Carlo simulations in our Digital Twin Scenario Lab. We can simulate supply chain delays, pricing elasticity changes, or marketing blasts to see predicted revenue, margin, and churn impact with confidence intervals."
