# MerchantPilot AI — Digital Workforce Specification

MerchantPilot AI replaces static administrative software with **7 autonomous digital executives**.

---

## Agent Directory & Responsibilities

### 👑 Athena — Chief of Staff & Strategic Executive
- **Department:** Executive Strategy
- **Role:** Coordinates multi-agent consensus, resolves debate deadlocks, issues morning briefings, and balances portfolio risk.
- **Key Metrics:** Business Health Score (0-100), Portfolio GMV Lift.

### 📦 Atlas — Inventory & Supply Chain Specialist
- **Department:** Supply Chain & Warehouse
- **Role:** Monitors SKU sales velocity, calculates safety stock levels, auto-drafts supplier POs, and flags delay risks.
- **Key Metrics:** Out-of-Stock Reduction %, Stockout Lead Time.

### 🏷️ Vega — Dynamic Pricing & Elasticity Engine
- **Department:** Pricing Intelligence
- **Role:** Scrapes competitor pricing, executes Monte Carlo price elasticity curves, and proposes gross-margin-maximizing price tags.
- **Key Metrics:** Price Elasticity Coefficient, Gross Margin Lift %.

### 🛡️ Sentinel — Ingress Fraud Interceptor
- **Department:** Security & Fraud Risk
- **Role:** Evaluates real-time order ingress against IP/VPN databases, velocity checks, and billing mismatches to place automated holds.
- **Key Metrics:** Fraud Interception Rate %, False Positive Rate %.

### ⚡ Pulse — Payment & Checkout Intelligence
- **Department:** Finance & Payments
- **Role:** Monitors payment gateway SLAs (Razorpay, Cashfree, PayU) and executes automated failover routing upon error spikes.
- **Key Metrics:** Checkout Conversion Lift %, Gateway Uptime %.

### 🎯 Orion — Customer Retention & LTV Specialist
- **Department:** Customer Intelligence (CRM)
- **Role:** Segments customers via RFM matrix, identifies churning VIP buyers, and triggers tailored retention offers.
- **Key Metrics:** VIP Retention Rate %, Customer Lifetime Value (LTV).

### 🚀 Nova — Marketing & Campaign Specialist
- **Department:** Growth & Promotion
- **Role:** Automates promotional coupon blasts, optimizes WhatsApp campaign schedules, and tracks click-through rates (CTR).
- **Key Metrics:** Campaign ROI, Conversion Rate (CR %).
